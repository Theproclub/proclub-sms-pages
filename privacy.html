Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

We do not share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Who we are

This website (theproclub.us) is operated by Gasman Insurance Group, LLC, doing business as GM Insurance Group and The PRO Club ("we", "us"), a life and health insurance agency operating in the United States through licensed agents. Contact: info@gminsurancegrp.com · +1 (832) 951-5888 · Texas 77384, United States.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us in our forms: name, email address, phone number, and state of residence.
  • Your consent choices, including whether you agreed to receive SMS text messages, with the date, time and IP address of that consent.
  • Basic technical data: IP address, browser and device type.

We do not request Social Security numbers, driver's license numbers, or bank or card details on this website.

How we use your information

  • To have a licensed agent contact you about the insurance information you requested.
  • To send the SMS text messages you agreed to receive: updates about your life insurance information request, appointment reminders, quote follow-ups and customer-service notifications from licensed agents.
  • To prepare quotes, schedule appointments and provide customer support.
  • To keep records of your consent and to comply with the law.

How we share your information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

We share information only with: (a) the licensed agent assigned to you; (b) insurance carriers, only if you choose to apply for a policy; (c) service providers that operate our systems on our behalf (for example, our CRM and our messaging platform), who are contractually required to protect it and may not use it for any other purpose; and (d) authorities when required by law.

SMS / text messaging privacy

Mobile information, including phone numbers and SMS opt-in consent data, will not be shared with or sold to any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. See our Terms & Conditions.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect your information. We keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop contacting you, by writing to info@gminsurancegrp.com or calling +1 (832) 951-5888. Texas residents and residents of other states may have additional rights under applicable state law.

Children

This website is not directed to individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
